Start from the problem

Start from the problem, not the device name.

The problem describes what happened. The device only decides the tools.

Pick the closest description, and we'll start from the right information instead of guessing.

  • 01

    Hit by a ransomware attack

    Isolate the device from the network and keep a sample of the ransom note before any change.

  • 02

    The device doesn't appear

    The fault may be logical, electrical or mechanical. Don't keep powering it on before diagnosis.

  • 03

    Files disappeared or were deleted

    Stop using the device so new data isn't written over the old files.

  • 04

    Water or shock damage

    Disconnect power and don't try to dry or run it. Physical damage needs a specialist inspection.

  • 05

    A server or RAID stopped

    Keep the disk order and don't rebuild before assessing the array structure.

  • 06

    Recordings were deleted

    DVR and NVR disks can be inspected and footage recovered based on overwrite state.

You don't need to know the technical fault name. Describing what happened is enough.
