Privacy & Cookie Policy.

This page briefly explains how we use cookies and basic data when you visit the site.

Last updated: 22 July 2026

Scope of this policy

This page explains how the Zero 2 One Data Recovery website uses cookies and basic data while you browse the site.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files your browser stores when you visit a website. They help the site work correctly and let us measure its performance.

How we use them

  • Essential cookies needed to run the site and display it correctly.
  • Analytics cookies to understand how visitors use the site, in an aggregated way that does not identify you.

Google services

We may use Google services such as Google Analytics or Google advertising. These services can set their own cookies to measure traffic or show relevant ads, and are governed by Google's privacy policy.

  • How Google uses cookies →
  • Google Privacy Policy →

How to control them

You can delete or block cookies at any time from your browser settings. You can also manage Google ads or opt out of Google Analytics through the links below:

  • Google Ads settings →
  • Google Analytics opt-out →

Data you send through the form

When you submit the contact form, we use your name, phone number, optional email address, and case details only to respond to your request. The data is delivered to our business inbox through the hosting and email providers; we do not sell it or use it for marketing.

To prevent abuse, the application uses a one-way fingerprint derived from the network address and submission times in windows no longer than 24 hours. It does not store the raw network address in the limiter file. Expired records are removed when a later valid submission is admitted; the hosting infrastructure may also record technical information in its security logs under the hosting provider's policy.

Questions about privacy?

For any question about this policy, contact us at info@datarecovery-sa.com.
